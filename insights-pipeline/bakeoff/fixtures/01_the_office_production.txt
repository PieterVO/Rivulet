The Office (American TV series) — Production

The Office is an American mockumentary sitcom that aired on NBC from March 24, 2005, to May 16, 2013. It was adapted from the 2001 British series of the same name created by Ricky Gervais and Stephen Merchant, and was developed for American television by Greg Daniels. The series depicts the everyday lives of office employees at the Scranton, Pennsylvania, branch of the fictional Dunder Mifflin Paper Company.

The pilot episode was closely based on the British original, and reviews of the first season were mixed. NBC ordered only six episodes for the first season. Beginning with the second season, the writers moved away from adapting British scripts and developed the American characters in their own direction, which is widely credited with the show's improved reception.

The series was filmed in a single-camera setup without a studio audience or a laugh track, in order to simulate the look of an actual documentary. Exterior establishing shots of the office building were filmed in Scranton, but the interior office scenes were shot on a soundstage at Chandler Valley Center Studios in Van Nuys, California. The talking-head interview segments, in which characters speak directly to the camera, were a device carried over from the British series.

Steve Carell, who played regional manager Michael Scott, departed the series partway through the seventh season in 2011. Reports at the time suggested Carell had hoped to renew his contract, but accounts of the negotiations differ and the exact circumstances of his exit have been described as difficult to verify. Following his departure, the show continued for two more seasons with an ensemble cast.

Rainn Wilson originally auditioned for the role of Michael Scott before being cast as Dwight Schrude. John Krasinski, who played Jim Halpert, shot some of the show's early establishing footage of Scranton himself while visiting the city.

The series finale, "Finale," aired on May 16, 2013. In it, the characters reunite for Dwight and Angela's wedding, and Michael Scott makes a surprise return as Dwight's best man — a cameo that had been rumored for months and was kept off the official call sheets to avoid leaks.
