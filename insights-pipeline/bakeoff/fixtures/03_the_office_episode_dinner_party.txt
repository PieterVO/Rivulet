"Dinner Party" — The Office (American TV series) episode

"Dinner Party" is the thirteenth episode of the fourth season of the American television comedy series The Office. It originally aired on NBC on April 10, 2008. The episode was written by Lee Eisenberg and Gene Stupnitsky, and directed by Paul Feig. It is frequently ranked by critics and fans among the best episodes of the entire series.

In the episode, Michael Scott manipulates Jim and Pam into attending a dinner party at the condo he shares with his girlfriend and former boss, Jan Levinson. Over the course of the evening, the deep dysfunction of Michael and Jan's relationship is revealed to the increasingly uncomfortable guests, who also include Andy and Angela and, later, Dwight and his elderly former babysitter as his date.

Much of the episode's comedy derives from small, escalating details of the couple's home: Jan's many Serenity by Jan candles, a tiny plasma television Michael mounted at the foot of their bed, and Jan's expensive Dundie award encased in glass. A running tension over Jan's work with a candle business and Michael's finances culminates in Jan throwing one of Michael's Dundie awards through the television screen.

The writers have said the episode was designed to keep all the characters trapped in a single location for nearly the entire runtime, heightening the discomfort, and that the confined setting was a deliberate departure from the show's usual office environment. The episode contains no talking-head interviews filmed at the office, another departure from the series norm.

Steve Carell has cited the episode as one of his favorites, and it was reportedly one of the most physically and emotionally exhausting to film because of the sustained tension the cast had to maintain across the long dinner scenes.
