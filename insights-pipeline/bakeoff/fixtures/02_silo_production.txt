Silo (TV series) — Production and casting

Silo is an American science fiction dystopian drama television series created by Graham Yost, based on the Silo series of novels by Hugh Howey. It premiered on Apple TV+ on May 5, 2023. The series is set in a future where the last known population of humanity lives in a giant underground silo hundreds of stories deep, and where citizens are told that the outside world is toxic and uninhabitable.

The first season consists of ten episodes and is based primarily on the first novel in Howey's series, originally self-published in 2011 as a short story titled "Wool" before being expanded. Rebecca Ferguson stars as Juliette Nichols, an engineer who works in the silo's mechanical levels, and also serves as an executive producer on the series.

The production designed the silo's interior to emphasize its enormous vertical scale, with a central spiral staircase that characters must climb or descend across dozens of levels. Filming took place in the United Kingdom, and the large sets were built to allow the camera to convey the claustrophobia and depth of the underground structure. Common lighting was kept deliberately dim and industrial to reinforce the sense of an enclosed, resource-limited world.

Apple renewed the series for a second season before the first had finished airing. Reports suggested the strong early viewership drove the quick renewal, though Apple does not publicly release detailed streaming figures, so exact numbers are difficult to confirm.

A recurring visual motif is the silo's relationship to "cleaning" — a punishment in which those who express a desire to leave are sent outside in a protective suit and, per silo custom, invariably wipe the exterior sensor lens before dying in the toxic air. The reason nearly everyone chooses to clean, despite swearing they never would, is treated as a central mystery of the first season.
