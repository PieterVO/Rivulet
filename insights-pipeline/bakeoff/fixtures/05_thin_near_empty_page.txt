Redwood Junction (2011 film)

Redwood Junction is a 2011 direct-to-video drama film. It was directed by Casey Marsh and stars
Danielle Ott and Robert Vance. The film runs approximately 84 minutes.

Plot

A woman returns to her hometown after her father's death and reconnects with an old friend.

Release

The film was released direct-to-video in limited markets. It received little critical attention.
